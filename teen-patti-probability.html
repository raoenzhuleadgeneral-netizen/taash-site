How these are counted

The total is C(52,3) = 22,100. A Trail is any of 13 ranks picked three ways from four suits (13 × 4 = 52). A Pure Sequence is 12 runs in each of 4 suits (48). And so on — every count divides cleanly into the 22,100 total, which is how you know none are missed.
