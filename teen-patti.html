The goal

Teen Patti ("three cards", also called 3 Patti, Flush or Flash) is played by 3 to 6 players with a standard 52-card deck, no jokers. Each player is dealt three cards face down. The aim is to hold the highest-ranking three-card hand at showdown — or to make everyone else fold before then.

Betting terms

Most of the game is betting, not card-play. These are the words you need:

Boot (ante)
The fixed minimum everyone puts into the pot before any cards are dealt.
Blind
Betting without looking at your cards.
Seen
Betting after looking at your cards. A seen player bets at least double a blind player.
Chaal
A normal bet to stay in the hand on your turn.
Pack / Fold
Giving up your hand; you lose whatever you have put in the pot.
Show
The final reveal between the last two players. The higher hand wins the pot.
Side show
A seen player asks the previous seen player to compare hands privately; the lower hand must pack.
Pot limit
A cap that limits the maximum bet to the current size of the pot.

How a round plays out

  1. Everyone pays the boot into the pot (say ₹10 each).
  2. Three cards are dealt face down to each player.
  3. The player to the dealer's left acts first — bet blind, or look and play seen.
  4. Play moves clockwise. On your turn you chaal (bet) to stay in, or pack to fold.
  5. A seen player may call a side show with the previous seen player to compare hands privately.
  6. When two players remain, either pays for a show. The higher hand takes the pot.

Popular variations

  • Muflis (Lowball) — Rankings are reversed — the lowest hand wins. High Card beats a Trail.
  • AK47 — All Aces, Kings, 4s and 7s act as jokers (wild cards).
  • Joker / Wild Draw — One or more ranks are picked at random to be wild for the whole table.
  • Best of Four — Each player gets four cards and makes the best three-card hand.
  • Lowest Joker — Each player's own lowest card becomes their personal joker.

Common questions

Does a Trail beat a Pure Sequence?

Yes. A Trail (three of a kind) is the highest hand, above a Pure Sequence — even though a Pure Sequence is slightly rarer.

Is A-2-3 a valid sequence?

Yes. A-2-3 is a valid run, and in most house rules it is the second-highest run after A-K-Q.

Which is higher between two flushes or two sequences?

Compare the highest card; if tied, the next, then the last.

What is the highest pair?

A-A with any third card. Between equal pairs, the third card (the kicker) decides.

Can you win without the best hand?

Yes. If everyone else packs, the last player left wins the pot regardless of their cards.

Is Teen Patti the same as 3 Patti, Flush or Flash?

Yes — these are all names for the same game.

How many players and how many cards?

3 to 6 players, one standard 52-card deck, no jokers, three cards each.
